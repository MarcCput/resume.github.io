Section 2: Personal reflection

1. What I did and what the artefact represents

My logbook records my daily stand-ups. In April and May 2026 it covers my design work: researching the SAQA process, planning the user journey and creating wireframes. In July 2026 (28 to 31 July) it covers the move from a design idea to a development-ready plan: presenting the Figma prototype, restructuring the backlog into user stories and planning Sprint 1.

2. Why I approached it this way

I used the three Scrum stand-up questions (what I completed, what I will do next and what is blocking me) so that each entry showed progress and impediments in a short, consistent format. The walkthrough questions made me check that all work was on the board.

3. What I learned

This week showed me that good software starts well before the first line of code. Presenting the prototype, restructuring the backlog, writing user stories and planning Sprint 1 gave the team a clear direction. My main lessons were to accept feedback early, keep the Trello board accurate and communicate blockers quickly.

4. Challenges and how I addressed them

The feedback on 28 July showed that our backlog did not yet follow Scrum standards. That was uncomfortable at first, but it was useful because we found the problem before development started. The impediment that kept returning was waiting for confirmation from all team members before development could begin. I learned that a project can be delayed by communication even when the technical work is ready, and that blockers belong in the Daily Scrum. In April my recurring problem was my limited understanding of the SAQA process, which I addressed by researching it (23 April 2026).

5. How it contributed to the project

The entries record the decisions that led from the early design work to a user-story backlog and a planned first sprint. When we were asked whether anyone was working on something not on the board, the answer was no, which showed that the board reflected our real work.

6. What I would improve

In the next sprint I wanted to update task statuses regularly, raise problems in the Daily Scrum as soon as they appear and make sure every member understands their responsibilities. I would also keep logbook entries for every working day of the sprints. [the logbook has no entries from 6 May to 27 July or from 1 August to 9 October: add entries if you have them, or say why]
