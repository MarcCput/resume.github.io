NavigSA Agile Portfolio

My individual portfolio for Project Presentation 3: five Agile components, each with evidence and a personal reflection.

NavigSA is a web platform that helps international students apply to study in South Africa. It was built by a team of four (Marc Kabala, Tanatswa Mabhodha, Witcha Francisco and Nolwazi Zulu). This portfolio holds my evidence and reflections for the five assessment components. Every page has two parts: Section 1: Evidence and Section 2: Personal reflection.

How the artefacts connect

  1. The charter set our purpose, roles and working agreements.
  2. Our purpose became user stories, each with acceptance criteria and tasks.
  3. The stories were ordered into the product backlog by priority (Must and Should).
  4. The team pulled work from the backlog into 13 weekly sprint backlogs of daily sprint cards.
  5. The logbook records the daily stand-ups, blockers and decisions along the way.

All five were managed on one Trello board, which is why the same story identifiers (US-01 to US-28) appear on every page.
